# 🌿 ELDORIA — WILDLANDS

*Somewhere beyond the familiar paths, a world continues to exist without waiting to be discovered.*

**An unexplored world. Creatures with stories of their own. Ruins that remember things nobody can explain.**

[🌍 Enter Wildlands](https://kadampushkar25-cpu.github.io/Eldoria/)

---

### The world doesn't reveal everything.

There are forests where unfamiliar creatures wander, coastlines hiding things beneath their waters, and forgotten places that seem to have been left behind by something—or someone.

A stone doorway stands without a building. An abandoned battlefield holds weapons and banners, but no graves. A silent beacon overlooks the sea, surrounded by contradictory stories.

Not every mystery has an answer.

And not every discovery needs one.

### Your journey begins in Willow Village.

Explore the wilderness, encounter creatures, build your team and discover the regions beyond the village.

But Wildlands is more than a journey of collecting creatures and winning battles.

It is a world of ecosystems, forgotten histories, strange occurrences and things that might never be fully understood.

### What awaits you?

- 🌲 **Explore:** Travel through forests, villages, ruins, mountains, caves and distant coastlines.
- 🐾 **Discover:** Encounter common, rare and exceptionally scarce legendary creatures.
- ⚔️ **Battle:** Challenge trainers, develop your team and compete in regional tournaments.
- ✨ **Evolve:** Train creatures, unlock elemental affinities and discover new forms.
- 🥚 **Build relationships:** Breed eligible creatures and care for residents in the Wildlands Sanctuary.
- 📜 **Uncover mysteries:** Record discoveries in the Uncharted Chronicles.
- 👑 **Find the truth:** Follow the clues leading towards the Forgotten Frontier and the mystery of Wildcrown.

### A world that keeps its secrets

Some locations are marked on maps. Others aren't.

Some stories are passed between villagers. Others contradict everything you've heard.

And somewhere in the Wildlands, there is something that stands apart from every creature you might encounter.

You may find it.

You may understand it.

Or you may leave with more questions than you arrived with.

---

### 🎮 About the game

Eldoria – Wildlands is a lightweight, browser-based, top-down creature adventure.

- No installation required.
- Play directly in your browser.
- Keyboard and on-screen movement controls.
- Local browser save system.

**Controls**

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| WASD / Arrow keys | Move |
| E | Interact |
| C | Catch wild creatures |
| H | Rest and heal |
| J | Open Uncharted Chronicles |
| P | Pause exploration |
| 1–4 | Use battle moves |
